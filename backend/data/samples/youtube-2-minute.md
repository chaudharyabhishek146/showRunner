# YouTube — the two-minute version

**Target length: about two minutes. Plan exactly three steps.**

A deliberately short sample. Use it when the walkthrough is the opener of a
call rather than the call itself — enough to show the product moves, short
enough that nobody has checked their phone.

## What this product does

YouTube has two ways in, and the whole demo is the contrast between them. The
**home feed** is recommendation-driven: the platform chooses. **Search** is
where the viewer chooses. Everything else — the saved queue, subscriptions,
history — exists to make the second one worth doing.

## The three steps

1. **Start on the home feed.** One beat on the default surface, so the audience
   has the baseline. Everything here was chosen for them.

2. **Search for a specific topic.** Type a real query and let the results land.
   This is the whole point of the demo: the same catalogue, reordered around
   what the viewer actually asked for.

3. **Open the sidebar and land on the saved list.** Watch Later sits under
   **You**, next to subscriptions and history — one click from anywhere on the
   site. That proximity is what makes saving a video worth the gesture.

## Concepts to explain out loud

**Intent beats recommendation, but only if it's close by.** The search box is on
every page, and the queue is one click from every page. Both are design choices
about distance, not about features.

**Saving is one gesture.** From a result or a video, a viewer adds to the queue
without leaving what they're doing. No folders, no naming, no filing — every
piece of structure is a tax charged at the exact moment the viewer least wants
to pay it.

## Out of scope for two minutes

Cross-device sync, downloads, playlists, Premium, and anything about the
recommendation algorithm. If someone asks, the honest answer is that this
walkthrough doesn't cover it — which is the answer you want in front of a
customer, and the one the agent will give.
